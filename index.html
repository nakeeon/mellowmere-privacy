Mellowmere — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Mellowmere (package com.woobadoo.mellowmere) is an offline farm game by Nazar Kaliuzhnyi.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or device data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data on your device

Your farm progress and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the game or its data removes them.

Permissions

The game asks only for vibration, used for gentle feedback during play.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
